# Druid battle skills: healing burst, pull, teleport, and rhythm

[Guide home](../README.md) · [Controls](../01-controls.md) · [Script index](../ScriptReference/README.md)

**Workbook areas:** `Skills`.

## Four abilities

Druid.cs supplies exploration floating. BattleController's `characterIndex == 2` branch supplies combat effects.

| Slot/key | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Basic / 1 | Radiant Chorus bursts at the aimed point, damages enemies, and heals living allies within radius. |
| First skill / 2 | Enrapturing Melody pulls initially selected enemies toward Druid over five sequence seconds. |
| Second skill / 3 | Resonant Passage bursts at departure and arrival, teleports to clear valid NavMesh ground, and resets movement origin. |
| Ultimate / 4 | Grand Ensemble reveals Mario/Pink, starts the shared shorter rhythm chart, and uses resulting accuracy for enemy charm/stun probability. |

## Damage and healing

Basic damage uses magic-adjusted `20 + round(Attack × 0.9)` and healing uses `27 + round(Attack × 1.13)`. Teleport bursts use magic-adjusted `14 + round(Attack × 0.7)` damage and `30 + round(Attack × 1.2)` heal. Burst applies each effect separately to live targets within its circle.

Teleport confirmation checks NavMesh position and nearby units. The displayed range clamps the aim. You cannot confirm an occupied/invalid destination merely because it appears inside the circle.

## Pull mechanics

The controller captures targets inside the initial radius, then repeatedly calls PullTowards. Forced movement also shifts their movement origin so the later range clamp does not snap them back. The five-second effect follows sequence speed, so fast-forward can shorten its real duration.

## Ultimate sequence

Grand Ensemble hides Druid's original renderers, spawns the performance model, reframes the camera, faces enemies toward it, and stops the introductory sound before the rhythm countdown. PinkRhythmController runs the battle beatmap and returns accuracy. Each living enemy gets a random test against that accuracy; a successful test sets stun to one, causing it to skip an enemy action. Camera and renderers are restored afterward.

Druid's ultimate unlock comes from Pink rhythm completion with at least 84,000 score (or `lastday`). The battle performance still needs its own live timing demonstration.
