## What WFC does

Wave Function Collapse chooses terrain while respecting allowed neighboring types. A cell begins with a set of possible tiles, called its **domain**. Collapsing chooses one option; propagation removes incompatible options from nearby cells.

## Your game's terrain IDs

| ID | Type | Bit mask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Grass | 1 |
| 1 | Water | 2 |
| 2 | Sand | 4 |
| 3 | Rock | 8 |

A domain can combine bits. `LandMask` allows grass/sand/rock; `DryMask` allows grass/rock.

## The algorithm in your code

1. Choose the seeded pool pattern for a chunk independently of collapse retries.
2. Begin other cells with LandMask.
3. Reserve the 5×5 water interior, a sand ring, and then a grass/rock ring.
4. Propagate all initial domains through the eight neighbors, including diagonals.
5. `FindLowest` chooses the unresolved cell with lowest weighted Shannon entropy.
6. `PickTerrain` makes a weighted seeded choice among allowed options.
7. Propagate again until every cell is resolved.
8. On contradiction, retry deterministically, up to 16 attempts; exhaustion throws instead of silently replacing water-adjacent cells with grass.
9. Instantiate `tiles[tile]`, the assigned prefab selected by WFC.

## Why the shore survives corners and chunk borders

Ring distance is `max(dx, dz)`, so the sand/dry rings cover corners as squares. Pool origins are inset far enough for both rings to fit within their chunk. Propagation itself stops at chunk bounds; the complete inset pattern is what keeps water away from an unsafe cross-chunk boundary.

## How seeds work

Awake chooses a fresh GUID-derived seed when `randomizeSeedOnPlay` is true. Disabling it uses the explicit seed. Chunk coordinates and salts derive independent random streams, so minimap query/load order does not change content rolls. Continue installs its saved seed.

## Visual blending versus gameplay

Noise supplies terrain preferences and blended painting. It does not replace WFC. `GetTerrainAt` reads the collapsed cell type, while `GetTerrainBlend` returns visual weights. The painter clones/paints TerrainData on instantiated terrain tiles. Resource, swim, and surface queries must use the WFC type.
