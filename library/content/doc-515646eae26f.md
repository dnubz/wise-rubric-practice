# RandaL battle skills: lock-on, SP buff, portal, and barrage

[Guide home](../README.md) · [Controls](../01-controls.md) · [Script index](../ScriptReference/README.md)

**Workbook areas:** `Skills`.

## Four abilities

RandaL.cs owns exploration book placement. BattleController's `characterIndex == 1` branches own the battle effects.

| Slot/key | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Basic / 1 | Locks a live enemy in range. A/D cycles candidates. Fires a projectile with magic-adjusted `10 + Attack`. |
| First skill / 2 | Restores SP to living allies in radius and adds a two-turn magic buff. |
| Second skill / 3 | Opens portal state and reduces the other three cooldown slots by one, never below zero. |
| Ultimate / 4 | Arcane Barrage enters first person for up to ten seconds. Hold/release left mouse to charge/fire. |

## Buff and portal rules

Ordinary first skill restores rounded `(5 + Attack) × 1.6` SP and grants a 30% magic buff. Portal-empowered use multiplies that restoration by 1.5 and grants 40%. The helper `Magic` applies the actor's current magicBuff multiplier to raw magic damage.

Portal empowers the next non-portal ability and is then consumed. Empowered basic projectiles can bounce through up to two additional nearby living enemies. Portal reduces RandaL's own other cooldowns; it does not loop over every party member's cooldowns. Explain this concrete scope when demonstrating the rubric's “Lower all cooldown by 1.”

## Arcane Barrage

The camera becomes first person, a crosshair appears, and party visuals become partly transparent. Charge caps at 1.5 seconds. Releasing launches toward the center-camera ray, with size/damage interpolated by charge and a shot interval of 0.5 seconds. A near-full portal-empowered charge enables bounce behavior.

At completion the controller waits for outstanding shots, stops charge sound, hides the crosshair, restores third-person camera, and restores ally visibility. Opening the menu pauses the sequence/charge audio until resumed.

RandaL's ultimate has no HasUltimate requirement in `CanUse`; the unlock check explicitly exempts selected character index 1. AP/SP/cooldown still apply.
